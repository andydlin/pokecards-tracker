How to read this
  • Sold history: PriceCharting aggregates eBay solds and shows the ~30 most recent per card (headline prices, before shipping); graded slabs and mis-titled cards were filtered out by title/photo. Live eBay sold checks cover the last ~90 days. Full data: sold-history-2026.csv (154 sales) and sold-monthly-2026.csv.
  • Coverage limits: only Glaceon VMAX and Glaceon V have readable sales back to January. Charizard V / Sylveon V start in June; the Terastal Gathering cards released Jun 12, 2026 and their readable raw record is October only; the Pikachu promo has just 7 sales (Sep–Oct) plus Terapeak volume; the Eevee set's history comes from Terapeak grouped rows.
  • Charizard rule: titles lie — a NEW lowest Charizard listing is photo-verified as the 132/127 alt art before it counts (the standing verified lowest is spot-checked weekly), and your $88 TCGcard-China listing is watched for selling/ending/repricing.
  • Card images: official artwork (English/Japanese printings of the same art) via Bulbagarden Archives. The Pikachu 066/S-P VW promo has no official image available anywhere, so it shows a placeholder instead of an eBay photo.
  • Prices refresh daily (~9:20am PT) in this chat's side thread. Browsing only — nothing is bought or bid on.
